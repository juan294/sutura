## Acceptance

Automated: Final release evidence has no missing/failed/skipped required ID; its numeric claims regenerate from exact artifacts. Typecheck, lint, tests, build, bundle, install, matrix, submission contracts, link/secret checks and media metadata gates pass in sequence. The video is under 180 seconds; caption/transcript timing matches the final file. Immutable hashes and release identities match across public artifacts.

Manual: Signed-out desktop/mobile Case Lab, npm/Marketplace install, public evidence downloads, video/captions and Devpost preview work. The description shows actual limitations. The submitted entry is publicly readable and matches the reviewed payload; a draft or confirmation click alone is not completion. The judging readiness manifest names owner, finite reserve or explicit pending blocker, stable archive, access check, credential schedule and monitoring; December observations are phase 7.

Any post-freeze behavior change creates a new candidate and invalidates affected evidence. If a public link or gate fails, repair and recheck before submission. Do not fill unknown evidence with a placeholder.
