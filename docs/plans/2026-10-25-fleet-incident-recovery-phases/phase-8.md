# Phase 8 — Field recovery and external adoption evidence

Parent: [fleet incident recovery](../2026-10-25-fleet-incident-recovery.md). Entry: accepted rollout with exact installed inventory and an agreed observation start. This phase adds no automatic spending, model changes or publication authority.

## Measurement contract

Use the existing collector cadence; its current local schedule is recorded privately. Required freshness remains 36 hours unless explicitly changed through Sutura coordination. Generate queue on every successful collection; independently evaluate one owner email digest per ISO week (Europe/Madrid) after each scheduled collection attempt through the phase 6 delivery contract; retain stale/partial output with timestamps when collection fails. The local collector owns scheduling; the authorized email adapter owns delivery. A rendered file is not delivery evidence.

Observe for 14 consecutive days after the last accepted wave, retaining later corrections as explicit window changes. Track per repository/workflow and failure class:

- source failures and independent incidents, attempts and recurrence groups;
- desired coverage, watched coverage, eligible events, admitted/declined/unknown events, setup/reproduction/search stages;
- matched, mismatched and unavailable reproduction evidence;
- typed fixed outcomes, qualified exact-head proposals, authorized integrated recovery, interactive/other recovery and unmeasured attribution separately;
- repeated known defects per applicable opportunity, duplicate Sutura presentation transitions, unresolved age and owner interventions actually recorded;
- measured inference/sandbox cost and missing-cost coverage; cost per verified recovery is undefined when there are none;
- full/fast verification contract, source volume and censoring, so changed cadence or partial months do not manufacture improvement.

Preserve baseline uncertainty. Historical next-green time remains descriptive, not owner labor or savings. Seeded benchmarks and interactive session fixes are separate cohorts from organic autonomous repairs. No denominator excludes failed setup, unknown cases or declined incidents silently.

## Acceptance levels

1. **Local implementation complete:** phases 1–6 gates and all confirmed source fixes pass; unresolved external/support limitations are explicit. This label alone says nothing about hosted operation.
2. **Installation verified:** every approved consumer has exact default-branch pin/configuration readback and required native checks. Unchanged excluded consumers stay excluded with reason.
3. **Repair path demonstrated:** at least one organic supported incident has matched native reproduction, audited fixed result, reviewable PR and all required native checks on exact repair/tested identity. Integrated recovery additionally needs the authorized merge and matching integration checks. At least one integrated organic recovery is required for the overall delivery objective; a green wrapper or seeded demo cannot substitute.
4. **Observed recurrence control:** 14-day fresh collection has no missed eligible watched failure and no recurring repaired defect over observed applicable opportunities. Report opportunities by defect; a defect with zero opportunities remains unexercised. A repeated repaired defect reopens the queue and blocks claiming that defect solved. Unsupported or externally blocked defects remain unresolved even if their monitor is quiet.

These are evidence levels, not a universal fleet reliability claim. If no organic incident arrives or there are insufficient opportunities, complete the observation report as `PARTIAL`/`unmeasured` and keep the objective open; do not manufacture failures, buy runs or prolong a foreground session indefinitely. The next normal collection/event is the resumption condition.

## Existing external installations

Maintain the separately named cohort in the ignored operational annex; public plans and fixtures use synthetic identities. Observe this cohort from original enrollment through the captured cutoff, split by installed Action SHA and source/attempt identity, alongside the owner fleet. Carry baseline observations forward even if a repository becomes inactive or remains on an old pin. A historical result under an older Action cannot prove the current development candidate fails; it does expose the need to assess, upgrade and verify that installation.

Report installed repositories, CI opportunities, eligible failed watched runs, monitor deliveries/skips with evidenced reasons, preparation passes/stops, reproduction attempts, proposals, complete native checks and integrated recovery. Zero opportunities means unexercised; missed eligible source events are a separate defect. Unknown skip reason, expired logs or inaccessible repositories preserve unknown/completeness flags. Do not infer human awareness, unaided installation, zero diagnostic value or overall repair absence from run colors alone. Narrow terminal logs may establish no repair in those specific attempts; developer value/comprehension needs feedback.

Acceptance requires an evidence-based disposition and visible next action for each enrolled external installation, including unexercised and unknown cases; no per-repository organic failure is required. Capture a sanitized generic regression from confirmed preparation failures, preserving version and failure stage. When authorized to upgrade an external consumer, verify the formerly blocked stage under the new exact candidate before claiming improvement. Existing third-party ownership, deployment and access boundaries still govern publication; membership in this cohort authorizes no changes or contact. Do not merge these assisted/historical installations into the unfamiliar-newcomer study denominator.

Add cohort tests for assisted installation, old/new pin comparison, all-skipped/no-opportunity, unknown skip reason, inaccessible history, missed source event and repeated preparation stop with zero triage. Every repository remains represented; no failure is dropped because a newer version is available.

## Independent adoption acceptance

Reuse the study and attempt instruments identified in `docs/research/2026-10-25-external-adoption-assessment.md:87` and the phase 5 fixtures. Prepare frozen instructions, scripts and synthetic rehearsal locally. Recruitment, contacting people, processing their repositories and paid trials need separately scoped authorization after that package is reviewable. This plan does not send invitations.

Enroll unfamiliar-to-builder repositories covering a supported Node project, a supported Python project and an intentionally unsupported environment. Use the immutable released public package/Action, not a developer checkout. Retain every attempted, blocked, failed, withdrawn and assisted session in the denominator; the three accepted outcome records in `scripts/adoption-study.mjs:311` are a coverage artifact, not an adoption rate. Record all manual configuration and author interventions, permission/provider provisioning and cost. Use two clocks: elapsed time from interest and active setup time.

Proposed release-quality targets: supported newcomers obtain a useful correctly understood result within 30 active minutes without undocumented author intervention; unsupported users get the correct limitation and next action before paid execution; four of five independent reviewers identify verdict, evidence limit and next action in 60 seconds. A useful readiness/diagnosis outcome can meet onboarding acceptance, but it does not meet repair-yield acceptance. At least one genuine supported repair needs independent complete exact-head native-green evidence before claiming that path works for outsiders. No study results or a too-small completed cohort means external adoption remains `unmeasured`/`PARTIAL`, not validated by fleet success. Update the dated “no participant study planned” evaluation position only when the new study is actually authorized.

Include interrupted setup/resume, customized App-token upgrade and pause/removal in the journey. If access lead time dominates failure, record that evidence for a separate delivery-model decision; do not silently build a managed service. If comprehension or completion targets fail, record concrete defects and a bounded next correction with separate execution acceptance; do not redefine the denominator to pass.

## Automated acceptance and regression prevention

Add synthetic time-controlled collector/queue tests for missed-run detection, partial pagination, stale clock, empty windows, default-branch drift, moved Action pins, missing build/runtime, changed verification obligations, false green, recurrence and duplicate rendering after restart. Public aggregate exports retain no private identities or free text. Capture the defect-specific regression whenever a supported incident escapes; keep original failure alongside fix evidence.

The final report must be reproducible from immutable observations plus companion schema/version and installed-policy digests. Recomputing the same snapshot yields identical counts/group keys. A late valid receipt can upgrade qualification; a contradictory receipt invalidates it explicitly. Historical records remain untouched.

Run new collector tests and parent full gates for any behavioral change. Read-only observation/report generation does not require repeatedly rebuilding unchanged product code. Native checks are those produced by authorized ordinary activity; no blanket reruns.

## Recovery, manual review and durable handoff

Owner reviews whether the queue answers what is broken and how it will be fixed, and whether verified repairs reduced intervention. Stop treating an unhelpful digest as success merely because it renders. Any proposed model budget increase or expanded runtime support is a new evidence-driven work package, not implicit authority from disappointing yield.

Final handoff names installed/tested identities, private measurement window, evidence levels achieved, recurrence opportunities, resolved and still-open finding IDs, actual costs/coverage, operational owner and next event/review date. Preserve literal `RED`, `PARTIAL`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `unmeasured` and `scan_complete=false` where warranted. No blanket “fixed for good” statement is allowed while a required field-proof or named defect remains open.
